## Local legal review

- Review package summaries, contact CTAs, illustrations, the final price presentation, venue licensing and applicable advertising restrictions. Keep the original promotions out of public HTML, client data, social metadata, structured data and assets.
- Confirm age and identification instructions against current Thai rules and the admission policy of each venue. Section 16/1 of the Entertainment Place Act describes an age threshold of 20 for customers during operating hours and inspection of an official photo document showing age. The original Thai legislation governs. [Department of Provincial Administration, English translation](https://multi.dopa.go.th/tspd/official_letter/download/2657/EN)
- Approve the operator identity, business and venue responsibilities, conditions of payment, changes, cancellations, refunds and consumer disclosures in the three policy drafts.
- Verify the privacy notice against actual operations, including contact details, data recipients, lawful uses, retention, international transfers, access and deletion processes, consent and hosting logs. Check any GA4 property and its data settings before enabling optional analytics.
- Mark policies approved and add their effective dates only after review. Rebuild, inspect generated pages and search the public output before publishing.
