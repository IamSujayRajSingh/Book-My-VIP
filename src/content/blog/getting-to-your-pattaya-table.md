Before leaving for a Pattaya table, save the confirmed entrance, agree on a place where everyone can meet and choose how you’ll get back to your hotel. A driver may need a different pin from the one your friends use to find the entrance. Keep both in the group chat.

## Work back from the arrival time

Check the date and local time in your written confirmation, especially if the plan crosses midnight. Ask how long the venue will hold your table and who to contact if you’re delayed. Use a current route estimate from your hotel, then allow time to find your group and walk from the drop-off point. If the schedule looks tight, message the team before setting off. [How table confirmation works](/how-it-works/)

## Give each driver the same destination

For a group using two cars, send both drivers the same drop-off pin and choose a landmark on the same side of the road. Ask whether the driver can stop there on the night. Before setting off, agree on the route and how the fare is calculated. Thailand’s Tourist Police specifically flag unclear transport fares and routes as common sources of disputes. [Tourist Police travel advice](https://www.touristpolice.go.th/post/tpbnews2026103101)

## Make the return trip easy to find

- Save your hotel’s street address and map pin while you have a connection. Share them with anyone staying at the same hotel.
- Choose a lit, identifiable pickup point where a driver can safely stop. Check the car and registration against your ride details before getting in.
- If friends plan to leave at different times, give each returning group its own ride plan and enough phone battery to use it.

If the group needs assistance or translation during the journey, Thailand’s Tourist Police can be reached on **1155**. For a change to the table itself, use your original booking conversation. [Tourist Police contact information](https://www.touristpolice.go.th/en/main)
