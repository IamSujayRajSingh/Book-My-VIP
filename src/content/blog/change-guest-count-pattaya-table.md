You can request a different guest count for a Pattaya table. Send the change in the original booking conversation and ask for a revised confirmation. The space, price, inclusions and amount already paid may all affect what the venue can agree to.

## One more friend wants to join

Suppose six friends have agreed on a table and a seventh wants to come. Give the new total of seven, even if the extra guest plans to join for only part of the night. Ask whether everyone may use the same table, what seating is available and whether the quote changes. A guest arriving later may also need separate check-in instructions.

If the original table will be too small, ask what it would cost to move the whole group or use adjacent seating. Decide together before confirming an alternative. The [package comparison](/vip-packages/) is a starting point for that conversation.

## What if fewer people are coming?

Share the revised headcount and ask whether the venue can keep the original arrangement or offer a smaller table. Ask how the change affects a deposit and any unpaid balance. Splitting the original table total among fewer people can change each person’s contribution even when the venue charges exactly the same total.

## What if the group changes nights?

Treat a date change as a request for new availability. Identify the original date, preferred new date, number of people and the name used for the booking. Ask how long the original table remains held while the team checks the new night.

> Our booking is under [name] on [date] for [original count]. We’d like to change to [new count or date]. Can you confirm the revised seating, total and payment terms, and tell us which confirmation to use on arrival?

Keep the most recent confirmed arrangement in the group chat so friends arriving separately have the same instructions. Read the [booking policy](/booking-policy/) for the details to check before agreeing to a change.
