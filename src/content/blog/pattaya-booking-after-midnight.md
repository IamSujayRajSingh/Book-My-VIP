For an arrival after midnight, give the calendar date you will walk through the door in Pattaya and the evening you mean. If dinner starts on Friday and you plan to reach the club at 12:30 a.m. afterward, your arrival is **Saturday at 00:30**. Ask the team which event or service night the venue uses for that arrival.

## What should go in the form?

Enter Saturday’s date as your preferred date and 00:30 as your arrival time. In additional requests, write “after our Friday dinner.” Those two details resolve a common ambiguity in the phrase “Friday at midnight.” The [Book My VIP enquiry form](/contact/#inquiry) uses the Pattaya calendar date, including when you open it from overseas.

If your friends are travelling separately, agree on a date and time in Pattaya before sending the request. Copy the month in words into the group chat. “7 August at 00:30 in Pattaya” is much easier to interpret across different date formats than a string of numbers.

## What should the venue confirm?

Ask for the event night, calendar date, local arrival time, and latest time the table can be held under your booking terms. An after-midnight request still needs a venue-specific availability check. If two guests plan to arrive first, ask whether they can check in under the lead guest’s name and how the rest should join them.

## A message you can reuse

> We’ll finish dinner on Friday [date] and arrive at the venue on Saturday [date] at 00:30 Pattaya time. Could you confirm the correct event night, the latest check-in time and the name we should use at the door?

Keep the team’s reply with the venue pin. If dinner overruns, message the booking contact with a revised arrival time and ask whether the agreed arrangements still apply.
