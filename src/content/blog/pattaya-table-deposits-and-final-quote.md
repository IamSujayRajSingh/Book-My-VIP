Before paying a deposit for a Pattaya table, get the venue, package, guest count, full price, payment recipient and cancellation terms together in writing. The Book My VIP website prepares enquiries and does not take payments. Whether a deposit is required depends on the arrangements offered for your booking.

## How do the deposit and balance fit together?

Ask for three figures in Thai baht: the total agreed price, the amount due now and the balance due on arrival. Then ask which extra charges, if any, sit outside that total. If the venue says the deposit is applied to the price, the deposit and balance should add up to the agreed amount. Ask about any difference while you still have the quote in front of you.

For [VIP Gold](/vip-packages/vip-gold/), get both the guest capacity and cover arrangements confirmed. The public package price alone leaves those two questions open. Friends splitting the bill will need the same written total.

## Which questions belong beside the payment instructions?

1. Who is receiving payment for this specific reservation, and which booking reference should we use?
2. When does the quote or payment deadline expire in Pattaya time?
3. What happens to the deposit if the guest count changes, the group cancels or the venue cannot fulfil the booking?
4. Who sends the reservation confirmation, and what do we show at check-in?

If payment details change in a later message, verify the instructions with the team through the official [Book My VIP contact page](/contact/) before proceeding.

## What should the organiser save?

Keep the final quote, agreed change terms, payment acknowledgement if applicable, and the message explicitly confirming the reservation. Give the group the venue, date, arrival time and check-in name. Keep payment records in the organiser’s own conversation. The [booking policy](/booking-policy/) explains where venue-specific arrangements fit into that process.
