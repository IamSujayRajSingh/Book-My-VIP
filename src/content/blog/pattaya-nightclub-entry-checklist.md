For a Pattaya nightclub visit, plan for every guest to be at least 20 and bring official photo identification showing their age. Before you leave the hotel, check the venue’s accepted documents, dress rules and entrance arrangements. The venue makes the admission decision when you arrive. [Entertainment Place Act, section 16/1, English translation](https://report.dopa.go.th/laws/document/2/235.pdf#page=8)

## Ask which ID you need to carry

If you’re visiting from overseas, ask the venue whether you need your original passport or whether it accepts another government-issued document you hold. Check before relying on a phone photo or photocopy. Share the answer with each guest so everyone has their own accepted ID at the door. You can ask about a document by name without putting a passport number or scan in the booking enquiry.

## Check an outfit while you can still change

A term like “smart casual” leaves room for interpretation. If someone wants to wear sandals, trainers, shorts or a sleeveless top, name the item and ask whether it is acceptable for your particular booking. If you’re bringing bags from a day at the beach, check whether the venue allows them inside or has storage. Forward the reply to friends joining later.

## Check the whole route to the table

If a guest needs step-free access, ask about the route from drop-off to entrance, from the entrance to the reserved seating and from the table to a suitable restroom. Mention any mobility aid and the space needed beside the table. A photo of a booth alone cannot answer those questions. Request venue confirmation before deciding whether the arrangement works for your group. [Contact the booking team](/contact/)

## Save the details for the door

Keep your confirmation, booking name, party size, local arrival time and entrance pin together on your phone. Give a second guest a copy in case the person who made the enquiry arrives separately. If the group size or timing changes, ask the team to confirm the revised arrangement. [Read the booking policy](/booking-policy/)
