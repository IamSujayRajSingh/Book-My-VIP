Choose Walking Street if your group wants to meet amid the nightlife, the Pattaya beachfront if dinner comes first, or Jomtien if you’d prefer a quieter start. The quickest way to choose is to put three pins on a map: your hotel, your dinner stop and the entrance to your confirmed venue.

## Walking Street for a club-focused evening

Walking Street is a natural meeting area for a group heading out in South Pattaya. It can get crowded. If friends are arriving separately, give them a specific entrance or signed landmark, a time and the name the table is under. Keep your phone close when you take it out to find them. [Tourism Authority of Thailand’s Pattaya guide](https://www.tourismthailand.org/Destinations/Provinces/pattaya/469)

## Beach Road for dinner before the table

Pattaya Beach stretches roughly three kilometres, so “meet at the beach” leaves plenty of room for a mix-up. Pick a restaurant first and share its map pin. The northern end of the beach is the quieter choice if you want a walk before dinner. Check the remaining journey from the restaurant to the venue when choosing your table arrival time. [Tourism Authority of Thailand’s Pattaya Beach overview](https://thai.tourismthailand.org/Attraction/หาดพัทยา)

## Jomtien for a slower start

Jomtien has a long beachfront with accommodation along the road parallel to the shore. If your group is staying there, meeting for dinner close to the hotel can make more sense than moving everyone twice. Once you have the nightclub entrance pin, decide together when to leave Jomtien and where you’ll meet for the ride back. [Tourism Authority of Thailand’s Jomtien Beach guide](https://www.tourismthailand.org/Attraction/jomtien-beach)

When enquiring, include your starting area, date, group size and preferred arrival time. If a specific neighbourhood matters, say so before agreeing to a venue. [Plan a Pattaya table enquiry](/pattaya-vip-table-booking/)
