For a birthday table in Pattaya, start with the guest count and choose one moment you want everyone there for. A group photo before guests disperse is easier to coordinate than a string of surprises. Send that priority with your date so the concierge can check what the venue can arrange.

## Build the evening around the birthday guest

If the birthday guest would enjoy seeing a group photo on screen, choose a picture everyone in it is happy to share. Ask who should receive the file, which format they need and when to send it. If the guest would rather keep the occasion private, say so when enquiring.

The [VIP Premium summary](/vip-packages/vip-premium/) lists a preferred song request. Give the title, artist and a backup choice, particularly if a remix matters. Check whether the venue can accommodate the request during your visit before promising the group a particular time.

## Can you bring a birthday cake?

Ask the venue before ordering. Confirm whether outside food is accepted, who can receive a delivery, whether storage is available and whether there is a handling fee. If cake is essential to the celebration, arranging it at dinner first can simplify the table booking.

## Give one friend the organiser’s role

Choose someone who can meet the group, recognise the booking contact and handle the bill. Keep the guest of honour out of any surprise-planning chat. A short message like this gives the concierge something concrete to check.

> We’re celebrating a birthday in Pattaya on [date]. We expect [number] guests, arriving around [local time]. Our priority is a group photo once everyone arrives. Can you confirm the table size, whether we may bring a small cake, and the total for those arrangements?

Share the final plan with the group once the venue, arrangements and price have been confirmed.
